# Storefront (public website) surface brief

Scope: every public route (`/`, services, projects, contact, start a project, estimate, invite to bid, bid, trade partners, client intake, request confirmation, privacy) and the client account and portal routes. Mode: Persuade on `/`; Operate on intake, account and portal. Audience and job are in PRODUCT.md. Constraints: every route, state and workflow stays; no invented proof (testimonials, awards, statistics); the fictional-sample provenance stays.

Main tasks, in order: see what the firm builds and where; see finished and current projects with their facts; request an estimate or invite the firm to bid; apply as a trade partner; sign in to the client portal.

## Direction contract
THESIS: The public website is the card catalog of the schools and libraries the firm builds: an oak cabinet as the dark bands, the reading room's plaster as the ground, and every project a typed index card with its subject and place above a red rule, its facts in typed fields and the rod hole at its foot. It refuses the hi-vis safety-orange contractor template, the blueprint grid, and the previous steel site board (mono labels, numbered sections, grid lines, hazard tape).
OWN-WORLD: Reading-room plaster (#e7e4dd) ground; oak cabinet (#3a2817) for the hero, the utility strip and the footer; buff card stock (#f6efdd) for project cards, the firm's own card and every form; the catalog's red rule (#b2382c) under each card heading and as the current-page underline; the saved logo hue only for the one primary action. Public Sans for headings and everything read; Source Serif 4 for the typed card records. Corners 3 to 4px; no pills, no grid lines, no tracked capitals.
STORY: An owner or facilities client reads the firm's line and its own catalog card (service area, services, projects, markets, phone, email) on the oak band, scans the subject guide of services, pulls a project card to read its facts, and requests an estimate or invites the firm to bid.
